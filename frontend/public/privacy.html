Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026 · Operated by Nafees Ahmad (Proprietor), Bareilly, Uttar Pradesh, India

1. Who we are

Apnajoda ("we", "us", "our") is an India-only matrimony-focused dating application available at https://apnajoda.com. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

2. What we collect

  • Account data: mobile number, username, password (hashed, never stored in plain text), and profile photo.
  • Profile data: first name, age, gender, city, profession, religion, community, bio, hobbies, intent (marriage / long-term), and the "Intent Passport" answers (timeline, children, family, relocation).
  • Verification data: a selfie submitted for manual verification by our team.
  • Usage data: likes, matches, chat messages, call metadata, and presence/typing status.
  • Payment data: We do not store your card, UPI, or bank credentials. Payments are processed by Razorpay; we only receive a payment reference and status.
  • Contacts (optional): If you use "Find friends from contacts", your phone contacts are sent once to our server to match against existing members and are not stored.
  • Device data: browser type, device model, and approximate region (for security and abuse prevention).

3. Why we collect it

  • To create and run your account, and to show you relevant profiles.
  • To verify you are a real person (selfie verification) and keep members safe.
  • To process payments for Premium and Boosts through Razorpay.
  • To detect fraud, abuse, and violations of our Terms.
  • To comply with Indian law, including the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

4. Sharing

We share limited data with:

  • Razorpay (payment processing) — payment amount, order ID, and email/phone you provide at checkout.
  • Cloud hosting providers who host our servers, database, and media files under contract.
  • Law enforcement or courts when legally required.

We never sell your data to advertisers.

5. Retention

Account and profile data are kept while your account is active. After account deletion, most data is removed within 30 days, except payment records which we retain for 8 years to comply with Indian tax law.

6. Your rights

  • Access, correct, or delete your data from Profile → Settings → Delete Account.
  • Request a copy of your data by emailing nafeesahmadbly@gmail.com.
  • Withdraw consent by deleting your account.
  • Complain to the Data Protection Board of India if we fail to resolve your concern.

7. Children

Apnajoda is for adults aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe a minor has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

8. Security

Passwords are hashed with bcrypt. Data in transit is encrypted with HTTPS. Access to production systems is restricted.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date above reflects the latest version.
