Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026 · Operated by Nafees Ahmad (Proprietor), Bareilly, Uttar Pradesh, India

1. Who we are

Apnajoda ("we", "us", "our") is an India-only matrimony-focused dating application available at https://apnajoda.com. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

2. What we collect

  • Account data: mobile number, username, password (hashed, never stored in plain text), and profile photo.
  • Profile data: first name, age, gender, city, profession, religion, community, bio, hobbies, intent (marriage / long-term), and the "Intent Passport" answers (timeline, children, family, relocation).
  • Verification data: a selfie taken in a random pose we ask for. Our team compares it with your profile photos to confirm you are a real person. It is never shown to other members.
  • Usage data: likes, Super Likes and Sparks, matches, chat messages and the photos, videos and voice notes you send in chat, moves in chat games, your answers to the Daily Question, call metadata, and presence/typing status.
  • Location (optional): only when you choose to share your location in a chat, and only with that person.
  • Notifications: if you allow notifications, a push subscription for your browser or phone, used only to send you Apnajoda notifications.
  • Payment data: We do not store your card, UPI, or bank credentials. Payments are processed by Razorpay; we only receive a payment reference and status.
  • Contacts (optional): If you use "Find friends from contacts", your phone contacts are sent once to our server to match against existing members and are not stored.
  • Device data: browser type, device model, and approximate region (for security and abuse prevention).

3. Why we collect it

  • To create and run your account, and to show you relevant profiles.
  • To verify you are a real person (selfie verification) and keep members safe.
  • To process payments for Plus, Pro, Boosts and Super Likes through Razorpay.
  • To send you notifications you have allowed, such as new matches, messages, and your daily picks.
  • To detect fraud, abuse, and violations of our Terms.
  • To comply with Indian law, including the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

4. Sharing

We share limited data with:

  • Razorpay (payment processing) — payment amount, order ID, and email/phone you provide at checkout.
  • Cloud hosting providers who host our servers, database, and media files under contract.
  • Twilio (SMS) — your mobile number, to send sign-in and password-reset codes.
  • Resend (email) — your email address, if you sign in by email.
  • GIPHY — the words you type when you search for a GIF in chat. Nothing else about you.
  • Push services run by your browser or phone maker (Google, Apple, Mozilla, Microsoft) — they deliver notifications you have allowed.
  • Family share links: you can share your own profile, and a member you have matched with can share yours, through a private link that shows a limited card (first name, age, city, work, up to three photos, about and intent) and expires after 7 days. It never shows your username, phone number or religion.
  • Law enforcement or courts when legally required.

We never sell your data to advertisers.

5. Retention

Account and profile data are kept while your account is active. When you delete your account it is hidden at once, and you can restore it by signing in again within 30 days. After 30 days your account, profile, photos, verification selfie, likes and matches are permanently deleted, along with the photo files on our servers. Messages you sent stay in the other person's chat history, as with any messaging app. Payment records (plan, amount, date and payment reference) are kept for 8 years to comply with Indian tax law.

6. Your rights

  • Access, correct, or delete your data from Profile → Settings → Delete Account.
  • Request a copy of your data by emailing nafeesahadbly@gmail.com.
  • Withdraw consent by deleting your account.
  • Complain to the Data Protection Board of India if we fail to resolve your concern.

7. Children

Apnajoda is for adults aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe a minor has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

8. Security

Passwords are hashed with bcrypt. Data in transit is encrypted with HTTPS. Access to production systems is restricted.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date above reflects the latest version.
