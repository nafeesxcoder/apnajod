Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026 · Operated by Nafees Ahmad (Proprietor), Bareilly, Uttar Pradesh, India

1. Nothing is shipped

Apnajoda is an online dating service. Everything you can buy - the Plus and Pro plans, Boosts and Super Likes - is a digital service inside your Apnajoda account. There are no physical goods, so there is no shipping, courier or shipping charge, and no delivery address is needed.

2. How and when it is delivered

  • As soon as your payment succeeds, the plan, Boosts or Super Likes are added to the Apnajoda account you were signed in to when you paid - usually within a few seconds.
  • You will see them on the Premium screen in the app, at apnajoda.com.
  • Plus and Pro last 30 days from the moment they start and do not renew automatically. Boosts and Super Likes stay in your account until you use them.

3. If it has not arrived

If your payment went through but the purchase is not in your account within 30 minutes, email nafeesahadbly@gmail.com with your registered email or phone and the payment ID. We add it within 24 hours, or refund you in full within 7 working days, as set out in our Refund & Cancellation Policy.

4. Where we serve

Apnajoda is available to adults aged 18 and above in India. Prices are in Indian Rupees (INR).
