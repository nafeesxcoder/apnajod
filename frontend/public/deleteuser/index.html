Delete your Apnajoda account

Apnajoda · Last updated: 27 November 2026

You can delete your account at any time. You can do it from the app, or by asking us by email if you can no longer open the app.

Option 1: Delete from the app (recommended)

  1. Open the Apnajoda app and log in.
  2. Go to Profile, then tap Settings.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete account permanently.
  4. Confirm. Your account is then deactivated straight away.

Option 2: Ask us by email

Send an email to info@apnajoda.com with the subject Delete my Apnajoda account. Include:

  • Your Apnajoda username, or the mobile number or email you used to sign up.
  • The email address you want us to confirm the deletion to.

We will check the request and delete the account. We may ask one question to confirm it really is you.

What happens after you delete

  • Straight away: your account is deactivated. You can no longer log in, and your profile is hidden from other members.
  • Within 30 days: you can restore the account by logging in again. Nothing is lost during this time.
  • After 30 days: the account and its profile, photos, chats, stories and matches are permanently deleted.

Data we keep

Some information may be kept for a limited time where the law requires it, for example records needed for payments, tax, or safety and fraud reporting. We delete or stop using everything else. For full details, read our Privacy Policy.

Questions

Email us at info@apnajoda.com. Our grievance officer can also be reached at the same address.
