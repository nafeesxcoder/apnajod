Why Apnajoda

Most dating apps are built for quick swipes. Apnajoda is built for people who want a partner for life. Profiles say what each person is looking for, members verify themselves with a live selfie, and you get a few thoughtful introductions a day instead of an endless feed.

Made for India

Indian relationships involve families, traditions and values. That is why Apnajoda has optional Kundli & Gun Milan, festival themes, UPI payments and a strong focus on respect and safety. It is available only in India.

What we believe

Real people, honest profiles, and safety first. You decide who sees you, who can message you and when you share your number. Anyone who breaks the rules can be reported in two taps.
