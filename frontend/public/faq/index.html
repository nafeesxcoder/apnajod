Is Apnajoda free?

Yes. You can create a profile, get daily picks, like, match, chat and call for free. Premium adds extras such as weekly Super Likes and Boosts.

Is Apnajoda only for India?

Yes. Apnajoda is made for people living in India, with features like optional Kundli matching, festival themes and UPI payments.

How does verification work?

You take a live selfie in a pose the app asks for. Our team checks it against your photos. Verified members get a blue tick.

Do I have to share my phone number with matches?

No. You can chat, voice call and video call inside the app. Share your number only when you feel ready.

Is Kundli matching compulsory?

No. It is completely optional and hidden from your public profile. Only people who add their Rashi and Nakshatra see Gun scores.

Can I use Apnajoda in a web browser?

Yes. Open apnajoda.com in any browser on your phone or computer. You can also add it to your home screen like an app.

How do I stay safe?

Meet in public places, tell a friend where you are going, never send money to someone you met online, and report anyone who makes you uncomfortable. Read our safety guide for more.

Can I delete my account?

Yes. You can pause your account or delete it permanently from Settings at any time.
