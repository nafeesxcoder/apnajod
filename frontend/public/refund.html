Refund Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026 · Operated by Nafees Ahmad (Proprietor), Bareilly, Uttar Pradesh, India

1. Scope

This policy applies to everything sold on Apnajoda through Google Play in the Android app:

  • Plus — 30 days, ₹99
  • Pro (includes the blue tick) — 30 days, ₹249
  • Boost packs — 1 Boost ₹49, 5 Boosts ₹199
  • Super Like packs — 1 Super Like ₹29, 5 Super Likes ₹119

Prices include applicable GST. Plus and Pro renew automatically every 30 days until you cancel in Google Play.

2. General rule

Because plans, Boosts and Super Likes are digital services that start the moment you pay, they are generally non-refundable once activated. Exceptions are listed below.

Purchases made through Google Play are also subject to Google Play's own refund rules, and you can request a refund through Google Play as well.

3. When we issue a refund

  • Duplicate charge: If you are charged twice for the same item, contact us and we will help you get the extra charge refunded in full through Google Play.
  • Failed activation: If payment was successful but the plan, Boosts or Super Likes were not added to your account, we either add them or help you get a refund through Google Play within 7 working days.
  • Unauthorized transaction: If you believe a charge was made without your permission, contact us within 7 days with your order ID and we will investigate.

4. When we do not refund

  • Change of mind after purchase.
  • Failure to use the service during the subscription period.
  • Account suspension or termination due to violation of our Terms of Service.
  • Boosts or Super Likes that have already been used.
  • The blue tick not showing because your selfie verification is still pending or was not approved — the blue tick needs both Pro and an approved verification.

5. How to request a refund

Email contact@apnajoda.com within 7 days of the charge with:

  • Your registered mobile number or username
  • The Google Play order ID (starts with GPA.), shown in your Google Play order history
  • Amount and date of the charge
  • Reason for the request

6. Processing

Approved refunds are returned by Google Play to the original payment method, usually within 5–7 working days. Your bank may take additional time.

7. Chargebacks and disputes

If you file a chargeback or dispute with your bank or Google Play before contacting us, we will cooperate with the investigation. Please reach out to us first — most issues are solved faster.
