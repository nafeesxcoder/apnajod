Refund Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026 · Operated by Nafees Ahmad (Proprietor), Bareilly, Uttar Pradesh, India

1. Scope

This policy applies to everything sold on Apnajoda through Razorpay:

  • Plus — 30 days, ₹99
  • Pro (includes the blue tick) — 30 days, ₹249
  • Boost packs — 1 Boost ₹49, 5 Boosts ₹199
  • Super Like packs — 1 Super Like ₹29, 5 Super Likes ₹119

Prices include applicable GST. Plus and Pro do not renew automatically, so you are never charged again unless you buy again.

2. General rule

Because plans, Boosts and Super Likes are digital services that start the moment you pay, they are generally non-refundable once activated. Exceptions are listed below.

3. When we issue a refund

  • Duplicate charge: If your card is charged twice for the same order, we refund the extra charge in full.
  • Failed activation: If payment was successful but the plan, Boosts or Super Likes were not added to your account, we either add them or refund you within 7 working days.
  • Unauthorized transaction: If you believe a charge was made without your permission, contact us within 7 days with your order ID and we will investigate.

4. When we do not refund

  • Change of mind after purchase.
  • Failure to use the service during the subscription period.
  • Account suspension or termination due to violation of our Terms of Service.
  • Boosts or Super Likes that have already been used.
  • The blue tick not showing because your selfie verification is still pending or was not approved — the blue tick needs both Pro and an approved verification.

5. How to request a refund

Email nafeesahadbly@gmail.com within 7 days of the charge with:

  • Your registered mobile number or username
  • The Razorpay Order ID or Payment ID (starts with order_ or pay_)
  • Amount and date of the charge
  • Reason for the request

6. Processing

Approved refunds are processed back to the original payment method within 5–7 working days. Razorpay processing time may add 2–3 additional days.

7. Razorpay disputes

If you file a chargeback or dispute with Razorpay before contacting us, we will cooperate with the investigation. Please reach out to us first — most issues are solved faster.
