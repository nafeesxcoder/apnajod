How Apnajoda works

Five simple steps from signing up to a safe first meeting.

Open Apnajoda - it's free

1. Create your profile

Sign up with your phone or email, add 4-6 clear photos, write a short bio and say what you are looking for. Takes about five minutes.

2. Verify yourself

Take a live selfie in the pose the app shows. Once our team checks it, you get a blue tick - people reply much more to verified profiles.

3. Get your daily picks

Every day you see "Aaj ke 5" - a few introductions chosen for what you want. You can also browse and use match filters, including an optional minimum Gun score.

4. Match and talk

When you both like each other, it is a match. Chat, send voice notes, play a game together, and voice or video call - all without sharing your phone number.

5. Plan a meet up

When you are ready, send a Meet up plan with a public place and time. Read our safety guide before your first date.
