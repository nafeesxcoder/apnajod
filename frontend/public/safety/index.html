Online dating safety tips

Video call before you meet

A short video call inside the app confirms the person is real and helps you feel comfortable.

Meet in public, travel on your own

Choose a busy cafe or park in the daytime, and reach and leave on your own.

Tell someone you trust

Share who you are meeting, where and when. Message them after the date.

Never send money

No genuine match will ask for money, OTPs, UPI PINs or bank details. Report anyone who does.

If you lose money to a scam

Report it immediately at cybercrime.gov.in or call the national cyber crime helpline 1930.
