Kundli Gun Milan explained: what the 36 points mean

A simple guide to Ashtakoot Gun Milan - the eight kootas, how the score out of 36 works, and how to use it alongside real compatibility.

What is Gun Milan?

Gun Milan (Ashtakoot Milan) is a traditional way of comparing two horoscopes using the Moon's position at birth - the Rashi (Moon sign) and Nakshatra (lunar mansion). It gives a score out of 36 points across eight categories called kootas.

The eight kootas

Varna (1 point), Vashya (2), Tara (3), Yoni (4), Graha Maitri (5), Gana (6), Bhakoot (7) and Nadi (8). Together they add up to 36. Each looks at a different side of compatibility, from temperament to health and family life, as traditional astrology describes it.

What do scores mean?

Traditionally, 18 or more out of 36 is seen as acceptable, 24 and above as a good match, and above 28 as very good. Many families also look at Manglik status. Scores are a tradition many people value - not a guarantee.

Balance tradition with real compatibility

A great score cannot replace good conversation, shared values and mutual respect. Use Gun Milan as one input - and spend real time getting to know the person.
