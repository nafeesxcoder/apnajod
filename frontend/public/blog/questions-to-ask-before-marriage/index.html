25 questions to ask before marriage (and how to ask them)

Thoughtful questions about family, money, career and everyday life to ask someone you are getting serious with.

Open Apnajoda - it's free

Why these talks matter

Love grows from shared values. Talking honestly about the big topics early helps you understand each other and avoid surprises later. Ask gently, share your own answers too, and listen without judging.

Family and living

Where would you like to live after marriage - with family, on our own, or near family? How involved should parents be in our decisions? How do you see festivals and family functions? How often would you like to visit each side?

Career and money

What are your career plans for the next five years? Would you move cities for work? How should we manage money - joint, separate or both? What do you save for? How do you feel about loans and big purchases?

Everyday life

What does a perfect Sunday look like for you? How do you share housework? Are you a morning person or a night owl? How much time alone do you need? How do you like to celebrate birthdays?

Children and the future

Do you want children, and when? How would you like to raise them - values, schooling, languages? Who would take time off work, if needed?

Conflict and support

How do you handle anger or stress? What do you need from me when you are upset? How do you say sorry? What would make you feel respected in a marriage?

Faith and traditions

How important are religion and rituals to you? Do you follow kundli or astrology? Which traditions would you like to keep in our home?
