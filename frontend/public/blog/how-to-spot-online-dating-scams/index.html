How to spot online dating scams (and what to do)

Warning signs of romance scams and fake profiles, and the steps to take if someone asks you for money.

Open Apnajoda - it's free

The most common trick

Scammers build trust quickly, then invent an emergency - a hospital bill, a customs fee, a stuck payment - and ask for money or gift cards. A genuine person you have not met will not ask you for money.

Warning signs

They avoid video calls, their photos look like a model's, they move the chat to another app straight away, they talk about love very fast, or their story keeps changing. Investment 'tips' and crypto offers are a big red flag.

Check before you trust

Look for a verified blue tick. Ask for a quick video call inside the app. A real person will be happy to do it.

Never share these

OTPs, UPI PINs, bank details, Aadhaar or PAN, or private photos. No real partner needs them.

If it happens to you

Stop replying, do not send money, report the profile in the app and block it. If you have lost money, report it at cybercrime.gov.in or call the national cyber crime helpline 1930 as soon as possible.
