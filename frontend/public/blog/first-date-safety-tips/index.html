First date safety tips for online dating in India

Simple, practical ways to stay safe when you meet someone from a dating app for the first time - before, during and after the date.

Open Apnajoda - it's free

Before you meet

Talk inside the app first and have at least one video call. It confirms the person is who they say they are and that you actually enjoy talking. Look for a verified blue tick, and read their profile carefully - real people usually write real details. If something feels off, trust that feeling.

Choose a public place

Pick a busy cafe, a mall food court or a well-known park in the daytime. Avoid homes, hotel rooms or isolated spots for a first meeting. On Apnajoda you can send a Meet up plan with a place and time from the chat, so both of you agree on it clearly.

Tell someone

Share the person's name, profile and the meeting place with a friend or sibling. Agree on a time you will message them after the date. Keep your phone charged.

Travel on your own

Reach the place and leave on your own - by your own vehicle, a cab you book or public transport. Do not accept a ride to or from a first date.

Money and personal details

Never send money, share OTPs, bank details or documents with someone you met online, whatever the reason. A genuine person will not ask. Report anyone who does.

After the date

If you did not feel comfortable, you do not owe anyone a second meeting. You can unmatch, block or report in two taps. If it went well - take your time, there is no hurry.
